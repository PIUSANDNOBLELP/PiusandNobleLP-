# Accounts to create

We're getting ready to move the website from our test environment to the firm's
own accounts.

Please create the accounts below — the **free plans are fine to start** — and
send us the login details (or invite us as a team member where the service
allows it).

We'll handle everything technical: connecting them, uploading the code, moving
the content across, and putting the site live.

---

## 1. GitHub

Where the website code is stored and managed.

- Sign up at **github.com** with the firm's email.

## 2. Vercel

The service that hosts the website.

- Sign up at **vercel.com** and choose **"Continue with GitHub"** so it connects
  to the account above.

## 3. Neon

The website's database (content, team profiles, articles).

- Sign up at **neon.tech**.

## 4. Cloudinary

Stores photos — team photos and article images.

- Sign up at **cloudinary.com**.

## 5. Resend

Sends the website contact-form messages to the firm's inbox.

- Sign up at **resend.com** with the firm's email.

## 6. Domain registrar

Where **piusandnoblelp.com** is managed (the firm may already have this).

- Make sure we have access, or the login details, so the site can go live on the
  domain.

## What to send us

- For each account: **the email address used and the password** — or add us as a
  team member/invite, whichever the service supports.
- For Vercel: the GitHub sign-up above keeps the two connected automatically.

Once the accounts are ready, we'll take it from there — no further technical
steps needed from the firm.
